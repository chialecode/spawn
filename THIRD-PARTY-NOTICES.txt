1. Clean-Room & Zero-Copyleft Assurance
---------------------------------------
- All NuGet packages across the SPAWN solution transitive dependency graph
  (audited via every project's obj/project.assets.json) are under permissive
  licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, or Microsoft Win32Metadata build-time
  SDK metadata). Zero GPL, AGPL, or LGPL NuGet packages are referenced.
- Prohibited packages (including Reloaded.Memory*, SevenZipExtractor,
  Squid-Box.SevenZipSharp, Keystone, Memory.dll, MinHook, EasyHook, Squalr) are
  enforced as hard build/test failures by Spawn.Architecture.GateTests.
- External third-party tools that a user may already have installed locally
  (such as 7z.exe fallback extraction) are never bundled with SPAWN and are only
  invoked as external processes when needed.
